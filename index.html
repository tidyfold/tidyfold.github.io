How it looks

Why not just use Projects?

ChatGPT Projects group chats around one initiative with files and instructions, but they are flat and heavyweight. Tidyfold folders are just labels: nest them, move chats freely, and nothing changes on OpenAI's side.

What Tidyfold can see

  • Chat titles and their URLs, as shown in your sidebar, for the chats you file.
  • Nothing else. It never reads message content, cookies, or your session token, and it makes no network requests.
